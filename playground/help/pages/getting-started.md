# Getting started

New to the xDBML playground? This section gets you oriented in a few minutes.

## What's in this section

[**What is the playground?**](./what-is-the-playground.html): a one-page overview of what the playground does, who it's for, and what it can and can't do.

[**A 60-second tour**](./sixty-second-tour.html): a quick walkthrough of the three panes, the header, and how they fit together. Read this if you only have a minute.

[**Your first schema**](./your-first-schema.html): a short hands-on exercise that has you start from a blank editor, write a simple schema, watch the diagram update as you type, and try a few of the interactive features.

## Where to go next

After working through this section, you'll be ready for:

- [**The interface**](./layout-overview.html), with details on each pane and what the controls do.
- [**Reading the diagram**](./reading-the-diagram.html), explaining what the visual elements mean: crow's foot notation, container styling, nested-field indentation, and the visual cues you'll see in the diagram.
- [**Working with schemas**](./loading-examples.html), covering how to load examples, share schemas via URL, and recover from typos.

If you want to learn the xDBML language itself rather than the playground tool, head to [**About xDBML**](./about-xdbml.html) for links to the 5-minute introduction, the specification, and the examples gallery.
