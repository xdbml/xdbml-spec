## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainership is earned through sustained substantive contribution over a minimum of six months. The bar is not strictly quantitative — review quality, breadth of engagement, and consistent demonstration of project values matter as much as commit count.

Current maintainers may nominate a contributor for maintainership at any time. Nomination is followed by a discussion period of at least two weeks, during which other maintainers and the broader community may comment. Approval requires consensus among current maintainers.

If you are interested in becoming a maintainer:

1. Contribute consistently over several months — reviews, proposals, examples, tooling, or documentation improvements all count
2. Demonstrate familiarity with the specification and the governance model
3. Engage constructively in discussions, including ones where you disagree
4. Let an existing maintainer know you are interested; we will discuss readiness openly

## Stepping down

Maintainers may step down at any time by opening a pull request that moves their entry from Current to Emeritus. No justification is required.

Maintainers who become inactive (no review or contribution activity for twelve months) are moved to emeritus status by the remaining maintainers, retaining recognition but releasing commit rights. Returning to active maintainership requires the same nomination process as new maintainers.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Respond to pull requests and issues within a reasonable timeframe (typically within a week)
- Review contributions for technical correctness, editorial quality, and alignment with project values
- Participate in proposal discussions when their expertise is relevant
- Honor the conflict-resolution process described in GOVERNANCE.md §8.2
- Uphold the Code of Conduct in all project interactions

Maintainer activity is volunteer or employer-supported. There is no expectation of full-time involvement, and maintainer time commitments may vary based on professional circumstances.
