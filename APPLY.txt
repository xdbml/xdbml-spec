WHAT THIS ADDS
--------------
Renderer
  - Groups draw below the supertype: stem from the bottom edge to the top of
    the half-circle; cross for disjoint, bar just above the base for total,
    each dashed when unstated; one line from the base to a bus, then an
    orthogonal drop to each subtype. Geometry is computed at draw time from
    current bounds, so it follows a drag.
  - Several groups on one supertype leave its bottom edge at evenly spaced
    points, in declaration order, with staggered buses so two never read as
    one line. A relationship line attaching to an edge a group uses moves
    near the left corner instead of landing on the stem.
  - Relationship names (option, off by default): named Refs get their name
    at the middle of the line's longest segment; groups get theirs beside the
    symbol, left of it for groups in the left half of the edge.
  - Interactive mount: clicking a symbol selects { kind: 'supertypeGroup' }
    and highlights the symbol and its branches. RelationshipVisibility gains
    supertypeGroups (default shown) and relationshipNames (default off).
  - Auto-arrange (relational): each hierarchy is placed first, supertype
    centred above the subtypes of all its groups, levels stacked, extra row
    spacing for the symbols; everything else is arranged around it.
  - Group members come from the parser's resolveSupertypeGroups(), so the
    diagram and the diagnostics agree on what a path names.

Playground (minimal; the Display toggles and inspector are Delta 6)
  - Compiles against the extended mount API: groups shown, names off.
  - Selecting a group clears the inspector until its pane exists.

Example 14, supertype groups: the Appendix C.5 model, with manifest entry,
README row, and a new golden SVG. The 13 existing goldens are unchanged.

Tests
  - Renderer: +46 checks (all five notation variants plus the mixed case and
    aliases, stem position, one drop per subtype, reserved edges, spread on
    a shared supertype, unresolved groups, names on and off). Interactive:
    +4 (hit area, click selects, hide toggle, names toggle).
  - Test hook: renderer and playground tests resolve @xdbml/parse to
    parser\src (renderer\test\ and playground\test\parser-from-source*.mjs,
    wired into their package.json test scripts), as the playground's Vite
    alias already does. Without it, example 14 could not be rendered in
    tests until parser 0.5.0 is on npm. Published packages are unaffected.
  - Parser: the example suite reads the repository's examples\ instead of
    parser\test\examples. All 14 parse; the stale copies are deleted below.
