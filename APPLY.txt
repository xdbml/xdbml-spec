xDBML -- v0.6 Delta 7: composite unique key badges, inspector spacing
Base: Deltas 2 to 6 committed (d685908 "Prepare 0.6.0: release date").

WHAT CHANGED
------------
renderer\src\layout\layout.ts, renderer\src\svg\serialize.ts
  Each field of a composite unique key declared in constraints { } now
  carries a numbered purple pill: U1 for the entity's first composite
  unique key, U2 for the second, in declaration order. It shows on primary
  key fields too, next to P (pitstops: raceid P fk U1, driverid P fk U1,
  lap U1). A one-field unique key keeps the plain U circle. A composite
  `unique` entry in `indexes` declares an index, not a key: no badge, as
  before. New FieldFlags.uniqueKeys (number[]); the role-marker helper's
  parameter type is narrowed to the four role flags.
renderer\test\goldens\15-constraints.svg
  Only change: U1 pills on races (year, round), results (raceid,
  driverid) and pitstops (raceid, driverid, lap), and the badges after
  them shift right. Rasterized and checked by eye: no overlap.
playground\src\components\inspector\EntityInspector.vue
  Space between "Check" and the expression, and between "Primary key" /
  "Unique" and the field list (a margin, since Vue drops the leading space
  of a text node).
playground\help\visual-cues.md, entity-cards.md
  The U1/U2 badges; composite `unique` index entries stay unbadged.
CHANGELOG.md
  v0.6 renderer bullet describes the numbered badges.
