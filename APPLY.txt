xDBML -- renderer: automatic arrangement (after 0.6.5)
Base: origin/main e836600 (release 0.6.5). Built 2026-10-03.

Example 17 opened in the playground put claim_submission far down its
Container, a row below adjuster_note. Two causes in the automatic
arrangement (renderer\src\layout\auto-arrange.ts), which draws the first
layout of every diagram without saved positions:

  1. A related entity that found its preferred cell taken went to the
     first free cell of the surrounding ring, and the ring was scanned
     from a corner: the second entity of a pair landed diagonally, one
     row lower. Rows are as tall as the tallest entity of the diagram
     (claim_submission, 1188 px), so "one row lower" meant 1244 px down.
     The ring is now scanned beside first (left, then right), then below
     and above, and across a corner last.
  2. Container blocks were packed in rows of a width derived from the
     total area, which wrapped the finance block under the tall intake
     block once intake had two columns. The packer now tries each width
     at which a row can break and keeps the arrangement a landscape view
     (1.6:1) shows at the largest zoom, the smaller area on a tie.

Example 17 now opens as in your second screenshot: adjuster_note, then
claim_submission at the top of intake, and the three Containers in one
row.

HOW TO APPLY
------------
Unzip the archive at the root of the clone (C:\Repos\Hackolade\xdbml-spec),
then, from cmd.exe at the repo root:

    apply.cmd

apply.cmd copies nothing. It checks that e836600 is in the history of HEAD
and that no commit since then touched the delivery's files; that each
unzipped file holds this delivery's content; and that the working tree
holds no uncommitted change outside the delivery. It then runs the gate
(npm run check, mcp type-check) and prints the commit commands.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
renderer\src\layout\auto-arrange.ts
  ringOffsets(): the order nearestFree tries the cells of a ring (steps,
  then same row, then left before right and below before above).
  shelfPack() and packBlocks(): row width chosen among the break widths
  by the zoom a landscape view needs; ASPECT = 1.6.
