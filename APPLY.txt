xDBML -- positioning update: Apache Ossie (formerly OSI) and LinkML
Base: origin/main 56de507 (release 0.5.0).

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Open Semantic Interchange is now Apache Ossie (incubating). Every current
narrative uses the new name, with "formerly Open Semantic Interchange" at the
first mention of a page, and links to ossie.apache.org or github.com/apache/
ossie. LinkML is positioned as the closest peer at the schema layer.

faq.md
  - "What's the relationship between xDBML and ODCS, Apache Ossie, OWL?":
    the Ossie bullet now names what an xDBML entity supplies to an Ossie
    dataset (source table, keys, fields, join relationships, and the §24.1
    synonyms for Ossie's ai_context); closing sentence says "datasets".
  - NEW "How does xDBML compare with LinkML?", as reviewed, with your
    wording: "...materialization settings have no equivalent in LinkML."
spec\v0.5.md -- informative text only; no version change
  - abstract, §1.1 (out of scope), §24 intro: Apache Ossie
  - §30 layer diagram: "Ossie, dbt MetricFlow, Cube" (the full name would
    overflow the box; column alignment verified)
  - §30 table: Apache Ossie row with the dataset handoff; new LinkML row
    "Schema (linked data) | Peer at the schema layer; partial translation
    both ways"
  - §30 closing: Ossie in the "does not attempt to be" list, plus one
    sentence on LinkML linking to the new FAQ entry
  - references: Apache Ossie link replaces the old OSI repository; LinkML
    added
README.md, index.md, xDBML_in_5_minutes.md
  Ossie name (xDBML in 5 minutes drops "Snowflake's").
ecosystem.md
  desired bridges: "xDBML <-> Apache Ossie" (Ossie dataset definitions from
  xDBML entities) and a new "xDBML <-> LinkML".
GOVERNANCE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md
  Ossie name.
CHANGELOG.md
  one line under v0.5 > Changed > Spec, marked as an editorial update after
  the 0.5.0 release, with no new version.

Left alone: spec v0.1-v0.4 (historical), llms.txt and the MCP reference
(neither mentions OSI or LinkML).
