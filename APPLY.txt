xDBML -- 0.6.5, delta 2: grammar and parser (internal definitions)
Base: origin/main a68fbc2 (0.6.5, delta 1: spec text). Built 2026-10-03.

The parser reads the definitions { } block of spec 15.8, resolves type
names in the entity's scope, reports the conditions of 15.8.6, and inlines
internal definitions into a field imported on its own. The renderer and
the playground inspector do not expand a field typed by an internal
definition yet (delta 3): until then the diagram shows "billing Address"
without its fields, and no error.

HOW TO APPLY
------------
Extract this folder anywhere outside the repo and run, from cmd.exe in it:

    apply.cmd                      (repo at C:\Repos\Hackolade\xdbml-spec)
    apply.cmd D:\path\to\xdbml-spec

apply.cmd checks that a68fbc2 is in the history of HEAD, that the fourteen
edited files are unchanged since a68fbc2 and that the three new files do
not exist yet, copies the seventeen files, and runs the gate (npm run
check, then mcp type-check). It stops at the first failure and does not
commit. When it ends green it prints the git commands to commit and push.
No package or lockfile changes, so no npm install.

referenced-key rule of 11.17, whether the field sits in an inline object,

constraints does count. Internal definitions follow the existing
