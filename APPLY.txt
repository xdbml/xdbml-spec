xDBML -- v0.5 Delta 6: playground inspector and Display options for supertype groups
Base: Deltas 1-5 applied and committed locally on top of origin/main fb985b1.

WHY
---
Third implementation phase: what you asked for in the plan review.
  - an inspector pane for a selected group;
  - the supertype's entity inspector lists its group(s) and their subtypes;
  - a subtype's entity inspector lists its group and its supertype;
  - Display toggles for supertype groups and for relationship names.

WHAT THIS ADDS
--------------
Group inspector (SupertypeGroupInspector.vue, NEW), opened by clicking a
symbol. Header badge "Supertype group" with the group name.
  Identification  name; supertype (link); completeness and exclusivity,
                  "Unstated" when absent; the pair read back as a sentence:
                    total + disjoint        Every Party is exactly one of: ...
                    total + overlapping     Every Party is at least one of: ..., possibly several.
                    partial + disjoint      A Party is at most one of: ..., or none of them.
                    partial + overlapping   A Party may be any of: ..., several, or none of them.
                    one or both unstated    says which part is unstated
  Subtypes        each as a link, with its own strategy or "follows the group";
                  unresolved members shown greyed
  Materialization strategy (or "Unstated, the derivation tool applies its
                  default"), merge, discriminator, with a one-line reminder
                  that this is intent for a derivation (spec 12.7)
  Settings        custom and unrecognized settings only
  Note, Edit in source

Entity inspector: a "Supertype groups" section when the entity takes part.
  as supertype    "Supertype of legal_nature: Person, Organization"
  as subtype      "Subtype in legal_nature, supertype Party"
  both            both lines (Person in example 14)
  Every group and entity name is a link. The inspector forwards a `select`
  event to App.vue, so the diagram selection and highlight follow.

Display menu
  Relationships   + "Supertype groups" (half-circle), on by default
  Labels (new)    "Relationship names" (Refs and groups), off by default
  Both persist in localStorage like the other toggles. Turning names off
  does not light up the Display button, since it hides no relationship.

Plumbing
  selection.ts    { kind: 'supertypeGroup', groupName } and equality
  ast-lookup.ts   resolves a group selection; supertypeGroupsOf(doc, id)
                  for the entity inspector (both use the parser's
                  resolveSupertypeGroups, so pane, diagram and diagnostics
                  agree)
  DiagramCanvas   maps renderer group ids (supertype-group:<name>) both ways

Tests: playground +1 case (group selection resolves with normalized values,
unknown group resolves to null, supertypeGroupsOf for supertype, subtype and
both, selection equality); 51 in total.
