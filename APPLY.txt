xDBML -- v0.5 Delta 3: inherited attributes without derivation leakage
Base: Delta 2 applied and committed locally on top of origin/main fb985b1.

WHY
---
12.5 defined an ordered "effective attribute set" and built rules on it
that assume a physical shape: a root-first attribute order (a roll-down
column order), paths through a subtype reaching supertype attributes
(true under roll-down only), and a computed "effective primary key"
(preserved-hierarchy key propagation). Placing attributes and keys in
stored structures is the derivation tool's job, not the spec's.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
spec/v0.5.md
  12.5  Rewritten. A subtype declares only its own attributes; the
        supertypes' attributes apply to its instances without being copied
        or redeclared; a derivation tool places them per the strategy.
        Redeclaring a supertype attribute stays an error, with a reason
        that no longer relies on a computed set. A path through a subtype
        names its own attributes only; a relationship to a subtype uses an
        entity-level endpoint, e.g.
          Ref assigned: Assignment.employee_id > Employee [source: '0..*', target: '1..1']
        Indexes and records on a subtype name its own attributes, with no
        special rule. The ordering and the dk-marker paragraph are gone.
  12.6  Rewritten. A subtype without a key shares its supertype's identity;
        the key each stored structure receives is derivation output. A
        subtype may still declare its own key.
  12.4.1, 12.4.3, 12.7.3, 12.10, chapter intro: wording no longer refers
        to an effective set.
  12.8  Index row removed; redeclaration row reworded.
  12.9  Bullet about listing inherited attributes in a subtype's box removed.
  28.5  Retitled "Supertype chains": computed for the structural and
        redeclaration checks only; no attribute or key of a physical model
        is computed.
  31    Conformance item 13 no longer asks for effective sets or keys.
  C.5   Adds an Assignment entity and a relationship pointing at Employee
        as an entity; the "what an implementation computes" bullets become
        "what the document states, and what it leaves to derivation".
  "What's new" paragraph adjusted to match.

CHANGELOG.md: the 12.5, 12.6, AST and C.5 entries of the v0.5 section.

Parser probe (v0.4 parser, current main): an attribute endpoint on one end
and an entity endpoint on the other, with explicit cardinality, resolves
with no diagnostic from resolveNames or checkRelationships.
