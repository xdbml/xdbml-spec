DECISIONS WRITTEN INTO THE TEXT -- please confirm (details in chat)
  1. Version rule (10.10, 11.17): the new conditions are errors in documents
     declaring xdbml: 0.6+, and warnings in documents declaring an earlier
     version or none. Keeps the MINOR-version stability promise and leaves
     §1.2 principle 1 ("every valid DBML document is valid xDBML") intact.
  2. constraints { } allowed in Entity, TablePartial and Edge bodies.
  3. Entity and injected partial both declaring a pk: the entity wins
     (§17.1 conflict resolution), rather than an error.
  4. DBML `indexes` pk entry: its `name` is the constraint name.
  5. Normalized AST: every key and check becomes a Constraint node,
     whatever form declared it (28.6); the raw AST keeps the form.
  6. Relational targets: Oracle, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MySQL, MariaDB,
     SQLite, Db2, Db2 for z/OS, Teradata, Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery,
     Redshift, Synapse Analytics, TimescaleDB. ClickHouse excluded.
     Unlisted names count as non-relational.
